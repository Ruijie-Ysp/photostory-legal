This policy describes what the current version of the app actually does. When the behavior changes, we update this page and notify you in the app.

Short version

PhotoStory has no accounts, no backend servers, no ads, and no analytics or tracking SDKs. Photos, locations, and notes are processed on your device by default. Only when you choose to configure and use an online model does the relevant photo preview or text get sent to the service you specified.

1. Information processed on your device

Data Purpose Leaves the device?
Photo/video capture time, location, altitude, camera parameters (EXIF) Shooting review, trip organization, light and place analysis No
Photo image (thumbnails) Scene classification, subject identification, sharpness measurement, perceptual hashing No
Photo library contents Browsing, organizing, cleanup suggestions No
Location (in-app camera only) Write into photo metadata, analyze exploration place No
Microphone / speech Speech to text (on-device when available) Apple's speech service may be used, see §3
Explorations, notes, captions, corrections Save your content No
Face detection and face enhancement Detect faces and locally upscale/denoise them on device No

This data is stored in the app's local container and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

2. What we do not collect

  • No account, phone number, contacts, or government ID;
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), no cross-app tracking (so no ATT prompt);
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting, or ad SDKs;
  • We do not upload your photos, corrections, or explorations to our servers — we do not operate such servers.

3. Network requests that may occur

  1. Your configured online / LAN model. When you select an online provider (e.g. DeepSeek, OpenAI, Zhipu, Moonshot, SiliconFlow, or your own LAN server) and tap generate, the app sends:
    • a compressed ~1024 px photo preview (caption / scene description);
    • photo facts such as time, place, and altitude;
    • exploration explanations send only names, places, and reference text. The app shows the recipient and content and asks for your confirmation first. API keys are stored in the system Keychain and used only to authenticate to that service. That provider's own privacy policy governs its handling of the data.
  2. Terrain tiles. Only a 1° tile identifier (e.g. N29E091) is requested from AWS Open Data for SRTM elevation. No photo or precise coordinate is uploaded.
  3. Model downloads. Open-source model files you choose are downloaded from ModelScope; only the file name is involved.
  4. Apple system services. MapKit, speech recognition, and the system camera may use Apple's services under Apple's terms.
  5. Optional web search (off by default). If enabled with your DeepSeek key, only your query text is sent — never photos.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with any third party.

The only outbound path is §3.1: a third-party model service that you configured and chose to use. You send the data directly to that provider; we neither receive nor retain it.

5. Storage and retention

  • All data stays on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Terrain tiles, thumbnail caches, search indexes, and cleanup scan caches are local caches and can be removed at any time.
  • Deletions in Cleanup are performed through the system Photos API: photos go to Recently Deleted (30 days by default) and can be recovered there.

7. Your rights

  • Access / correct: everything is local and editable in the app.
  • Delete: delete records, use Cleanup, or uninstall the app.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off permissions in system Settings, or revoke AI consent in the app.
  • Contact: use the email above; we reply within 15 business days.

8. Children and family mode

  • The family mode (Little Explorer) is designed for parents and children to use together. It contains no ads, social features, or purchase prompts.
  • Children's content is processed on device by default; only after a parent configures an online model and confirms will relevant text or photo previews be sent to that service.
  • We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe we hold any.

9. International transfers

No transfer by default. If you configure an overseas model provider (e.g. OpenAI), data is sent by you directly to that provider — your own choice. Please make sure it complies with your local regulations.

10. Security

  • No backend to breach;
  • API keys in the system Keychain;
  • Online requests use HTTPS (LAN endpoints are configured by you);
  • Deletion requires both an in-app confirmation and a system confirmation.

11. Changes

If features or data flows change, we update this page and its effective date, and notify you in the app for material changes.
